Common Troubleshooting Steps

1. Bot Authorization & Server Access Errors

Cause: The account attempting to invite Global Reach does not hold Manage Server (MANAGE_GUILD) or Administrator permissions on the target Discord server, or moderator Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) is required by the server.
Fix: Verify that your Discord account has administrative permissions on the server and 2FA enabled before launching the OAuth2 authorization flow.

2. Missing Channel Access or Permission Denials

Cause: Channel-specific permission overrides on the target announcement channel deny View Channels, Send Messages, or Embed Links for the bot or its assigned role.
Fix: Ensure Global Reach (Application ID: 722756633499009045) is granted View Channels, Send Messages, and Embed Links in the target announcement channel, and that the Server Members Intent is enabled in the Discord Developer Portal when role-holder consent verification is used.

3. Campaign Blocked by Audience Consent Verification

Cause: One or more non-bot members holding the target notification role do not have an active individual opt-in consent record, or have previously opted out.
Fix: Global Reach fails closed whenever consent is incomplete. Ensure that all members assigned to the target notification role have completed individual opt-in verification and remove any non-consented role assignments before retrying.
